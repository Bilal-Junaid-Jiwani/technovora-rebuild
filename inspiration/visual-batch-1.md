## Locomotive (locomotive.ca)
- Hero: full-bleed red-lit photographic portrait, oversized teal-outlined serif headline "Locomotive®" + "Digital-first Design Agency"; black pill nav.
- Mid: Featured Work as gigantic elegant serif project names alternating left/right, separated by fine ruled lines — editorial, poster-like.

## Darkroom Engineering (darkroom.engineering)
- Hero: black canvas, giant red distressed-stencil monospace "WHERE THINGS GET DEVELOPED", dictionary-style "[ DARKROOM ], NOUN" definition.
- Mid: terminal-style ACTIVITY LOG with dated entries — changelog-as-design.
- Discipline: monospace everywhere; strict black/red/white; theme toggles. Engineered, not decorated.

## Cuberto (cuberto.com)
- Hero: ultra-minimal white, one centered giant grotesque statement "Digital design & development agency", single black pill "Contacts" CTA; zero imagery.
- Mid: Selected Work on solid black — 2×2 rounded case cards with one-line captions.
- Discipline: single grotesque family, extreme black/white contrast sections, no accent colors. Restraint as brand.

## Clay (clay.global)
