# Agency inspiration — 20 software/service agencies survey
Researched 2026-09-26 (homepage + inner pages fetched). Patterns only, nothing copied.

## Per-agency notes

**thoughtbot** — Video-led hero: autoplay case-study product clip beside sentence-case headline; moving CLIENT PRODUCT is the visual. Editorial case block: client kicker, discipline tags, one outcome sentence. HBR pull-quote with full attribution. Steal: lead hero with real client-product footage. Avoid: hero collapsing when video fails — design fallback first-class.

**DockYard** — Left headline + subline + bold CTA; abstract geo-wave backdrop. Services as stacked expanding text list. Work cards: full-width image + OUTCOME headline ("How DockYard doubled Kamana's user base") + discipline tags. Steal: outcome headline first, image second, tags third.

**10up** — Cinematic video hero, craft-forward statement. Featured-project horizontal list. Testimonial wall: long run of named/titled/company-attributed quotes (Microsoft, ex-WIRED). Steal: named-quote testimonial wall — every quote converts skeptics. Avoid: testimonial+blog+team bands stacked without breathing room.

**Human Made** — Mega-type staccato: "WordPress. At scale. For impact." + ★★★★★ Clutch badge under headline. 8-logo monochrome wall. Testimonials pairing quote + logo + photo/name/title. Steal: staccato period-headline + third-party rating in one glance. Avoid: same icon-grid mechanic twice in a row.

**XWP** — Blunt statement headlines. Six chunky service blocks, one-line pitch + "Learn more". Awards band anchored by ONE giant stat ("92%"). Steal: giant-number credibility band. Avoid: credential-stuffing.

**Netguru** — Full-viewport showreel, small headline. Five stacked H1-level capability blocks (editorial list, not cards). 4-tile outcome-metric band: every stat paired with client-result sentence. Steal: outcome-metric band — numbers with result sentences, scannable in 5s. Avoid: headline abdication — video + strong type, never video alone.

**Brainhub** — Centered human hero: portrait + "Painless team augmentation." Process timeline "Expect the first pull request in 10 days" — Day 1/4/6/10 roadmap. Steal: Day 1→Day 10 delivery roadmap — process as product with timeline promise. Avoid: 9 testimonials on homepage — cap 3–4.

**10Clouds** — Numbered editorial system: sections labeled /01, /02, /03; trust strip (4.9/5 Clutch, 17 Years, 120+ AI deployments, 72 NPS). Partnership cards with announcement links. Steal: numbered section indices as recurring nav device. Avoid: one vertical owning whole homepage.

**Droids On Roids** — Split hero: headline + stat tiles left, looping video right. Case studies as metric cards. TAP framework: branded method in 8 one-line principle tiles. Steal: named delivery framework in one-line tiles — process as productized asset. Avoid: duplicating metrics top and bottom.

**Monterail** — Left-aligned restraint: "Since 2010… 400+ clients" + TRUSTED BY logo row (Bosch, Merck, Polaroid). Case studies as outcome-headline H3s ("100 hours back a week") + tag chips. Single font (Basier Circle), signature red #F50031. Steal: outcome-headline case cards — portfolio becomes a list of wins. Dark/light alternation.

**Viget** — Text-led: "Building a better digital world." + "Happily independent since 1999." Work grid with "Category + client" kicker titles — portfolio doubles as services menu. Closing CTA is one question + raw contact block, no form friction. Steal: "Category + client" convention — 30 tiles scannable. Avoid: contextless logo walls.

**8th Light** — "We Ship AI." Contrarian voice: "AI makes it cheap to build. It doesn't make it cheap to be wrong." Uniform work tiles "Industry | Project headline". Case pages: Challenge/Partnership/Impact triptych before narrative. Steal: triptych atop case studies — CTO gets story in 10s. Avoid: AI-saturating every headline.

**Clearleft** — Community-first editorial, real team photos. Case features framed as QUESTIONS ("How do you begin to bring an ambitious strategic vision to life?"). Team page exposes capability columns per person — directory IS the services page. Steal: frame cases as questions. Avoid: cluttered mixed indexes.

**Clay (SF)** — Tagline hero. Public FAQ as manifesto-length positioning. "Content production studio" block. Steal: FAQ as manifesto — long-form Q&A as B2B positioning.

**Cheesecake Labs** — Stats strip above fold (13+ Years / 300+ Products / 4.9). "Positioning by negation": "Not a consultancy selling a 24-month program. Not an offshore shop…" Steal: anti-positioning negation block — disqualify competitors in 3 lines. Avoid: metric cards with no outcome narrative.

**Fueled** — Service-word tab strip (AI/Mobile/Web/Growth) above mega-type "Digital Done Right" over planet video. Case grid with verb-first headlines ("We Made a Community Mural with AI"). Steal: "We + verb + outcome" case headlines — portfolio as stories. Avoid: whole sprite-animation icon system.

**ArcTouch** — Vimeo bg video + "We help companies create the digital products people use every day" + "You are our Success Metric". Accessible testimonial carousel. Signature: footer CTA "Still not sure? Ask ChatGPT, Claude or Perplexity why ArcTouch is great" — AI-search referral buttons. Steal: "Ask the AIs" CTA — meets LLM-era buyer behavior.

**WillowTree/TELUS Digital** — Five service blocks, crisp outcome paragraphs. "Fuel iX" — capability productized into named offering. Engineering blog proving process depth. Steal: naming/productizing a capability + engineering blog.

**ELEKS** — Promise headline cycling industry×service cards. 3-question "match your needs" quiz in hero zone with consultation payoff. Case carousel as .webm video cards. Steal: 3-question needs-matcher quiz — qualifies leads while teaching taxonomy. Avoid: density overload — pick two proof mechanisms per page.

**Phenomenon Studio** — Case pages: Task → Solution → Result narrative spine. Steal: one idea per chapter, never a spec wall. Avoid: bespoke cinematic motion on every page.

## Cross-cutting constants (19/20)
Light theme dominates; ONE accent; sentence-case statement headlines; 1–2 typefaces; color as punctuation; work/product evidence above the fold.

## Top 10 steal-worthy patterns
1. Outcome-headline case cards (Monterail, DockYard)
2. "Category + client" work titles (Viget)
3. Challenge/Partnership/Impact triptych (8th Light)
4. Day 1→Day 10 delivery roadmap (Brainhub)
5. Numbered section indices /01 /02 (10Clouds)
6. Anti-positioning negation block (Cheesecake Labs)
7. Named delivery framework in one-line tiles (Droids On Roids)
8. Staccato period-headline + rating badge (Human Made)
9. Hero lead-qualification quiz (ELEKS)
10. Named testimonials everywhere — anonymous quotes convert nothing

## Anti-patterns
Contextless logo walls; video heroes with no fallback; duplicated metric tiles; 9 stacked testimonials; award walls; 9-field homepage forms; identical icon-grids on adjacent sections; one vertical owning homepage.
