# INSPIRATION DIGEST — Technovora rebuild, revision pass
Compiled 2026-09-26 from 40 agency sites surveyed (20 creative + 20 dev) + 20 screenshots.
Full notes: `agency-survey-creative-20.md`, `agency-survey-dev-20.md`, `visual-batch-1.md`, `visual-batch-2.md`.
Rule: take the PATTERN, never the copy, never the brand costume. Every page design must cite which patterns informed it.

## The 12 highest-value patterns (steal these)
1. **Logo-wall-with-outcomes** (Ramotion) — each logo gets a one-line outcome. Best B2B trust pattern found. (No fake logos — use tech-partner/press-style rows or "selected capabilities" rows instead; only real names.)
2. **Outcome-headline case cards** (Monterail, DockYard) — the card title IS the business result. Honest version: qualitative outcomes, no invented numbers.
3. **Services-as-tagline list** (COLLINS) — name + one punchy line, writing as visual design, stacked list, zero decoration.
4. **Numbered services index 01–06** (Huge) — capabilities as a table of contents.
5. **Per-section meta-bar** (BASIC/DEPT) — 1px rule + tiny uppercase label/value above sections. Editorial rhythm, zero decoration.
6. **Activity Log / shipping changelog** (Darkroom) — dated entries ("2026/09/12 — Shipped…"). Dated receipts beat claims. Keep tone professional, not jokey.
7. **Single featured story** (Work & Co "Latest") — one big story > grid of thumbnails. Quote-as-first-proof only with REAL quotes.
8. **Stacked capability bands** (Clay) — each service with image panel + 2-line plain-spoken description; services page as product tour.
9. **Objection-handling FAQ** (Clay/Cuberto) — 4–5 questions buyers actually ask (pricing, timelines, process). Cap at 5.
10. **Day 1→Day 10 delivery roadmap** (Brainhub) — process sold as product with a timeline promise.
11. **Anti-positioning negation** (Cheesecake Labs) — "Not a consultancy selling a 24-month program…" Disqualify competitors in 3 lines.
12. **Needs-matcher quiz, 3 questions** (ELEKS) — qualifies leads while teaching the service taxonomy. Keep light.

## Suggested page → pattern mapping (builders: adapt, don't template)
- **/** (homepage): typographic statement hero (Cuberto/Work & Co discipline — copy must be genuinely good, it's a copy-bet) OR left-aligned headline + honest build visual (thoughtbot: real product footage > abstract art). Sections: meta-bar rhythm throughout; numbered services index; single featured story; outcome-metric band (Netguru: every stat needs a result sentence — use only honest, modest numbers); Activity Log teaser; 4–5 Q objection FAQ; one-line-question CTA (Viget).
- **/services**: stacked capability bands (Clay) with visual panels (Cuberto), plain-spoken 2-line descriptions.
- **/services/[slug] ×6** — each page a DIFFERENT composition drawn from: chapter structure (frog), Challenge/Partnership/Impact triptych (8th Light), delivery roadmap (Brainhub), "We + verb + outcome" story format (Fueled), Task/Solution/Result spine (Phenomenon), numbered phases with own illustrations (10Clouds). No two service pages share a layout.
- **/packages**: Stripe-like pricing clarity; add-ons menu; guarantee band; payment-terms visual. FAQ = objection-handling (4–5).
- **/portfolio**: uniform-crop grid + outcome-led titles (Instrument); insight-first index (Fantasy: outcome line before name); single featured piece (Work & Co). NO invented metrics, NO fake detail affordances.
- **/blog**: studio journal, not marketing blog (Studio Output, Koto). 3 real substantial articles.
- **/contact**: compressed office row (Koto, one row not five blocks); raw contact block + one-question CTA (Viget); light 3-question needs-matcher (ELEKS) OR honest mailto-compose form. NO dead forms.
- **/about**: UNTOUCHED. Do not modify, do not restyle, do not "harmonize".
- **/privacy, /terms**: already built — keep.

## Hard anti-patterns (reject on sight)
Fake/contextless logo walls; custom cursors; preloaders; film grain; glow orbs; dot grids; 3D globes; auto-rotating carousels; manifesto copy without substance; video-everywhere (one loop max, designed fallback frame); emoji punctuation; FAQ longer than 5; keyword-stuffed sections; gradient-text headlines; ALL-CAPS mega-type; more than 1 typeface (Inter only) + 1 accent (#F97316, used sparingly).
